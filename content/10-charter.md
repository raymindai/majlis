# Programme Charter v2.1
`id: CHARTER` · `date: 2025-08-14` · `authority: Foundational (may be superseded)`

## Scope
Manarah consolidates priority government services from 5 entities onto a single unified resident platform, with a shared identity layer (Single Sign-On) delivered by EDD.

## Participating entities & initial budget allocation
| Entity | Scope | Allocation (AED) |
|--------|-------|------------------|
| HSA — Health Services Authority | Health services | 42M |
| EKD — Education & Knowledge Dept | Education services incl. Parent Portal | 45M |
| MTA — Municipalities & Transport Authority | Municipal & transport services | **40M** |
| PSD — Public Security Directorate | Security-related services | 38M |
| EDD — Economic Development Dept | Licensing services + **shared SSO** | 45M |
| **Total** | | **240M** (incl. 30M central) |

## Governance
Quarterly steering committee, chaired by the Programme DG. Each entity reports status and spend. Budget reallocations require committee approval.

> Note: allocations are the original baseline. Quarterly reports supersede these figures where they differ.
