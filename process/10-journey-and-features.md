# 10 · The Meeting Journey & Feature Inventory

The product isn't one screen — it's a companion that flows across the meeting lifecycle:
**Before → During → After**, where each stage feeds the next. The output of *after*
(decisions, new commitments) becomes the input of the next *before* (the prior positions to
check against). **That loop is the high concept: Majlis is institutional memory, not a one-shot brief.**

```
   ┌──────────── the loop ────────────┐
   ▼                                   │
 BEFORE  ──►  DURING  ──►  AFTER  ──────┘
 prep &       live          minutes &
 rehearse     support       commitments captured
   ▲                          │
   └──── prior commitments ◄───┘  (next cycle checks if they were kept)
```

## Walkthrough — the Manarah Q2 steering meeting, through the DG's day

**Before (night before / that morning).** The DG opens Majlis to an assembled brief: the 5 entities at a glance, the headline risk (EDD's SSO slip blocking HSA + EKD), the decision on the table (budget reallocation), and who's in the room. They ask: *"Did EKD meet its March commitment?"* → grounded, cited, **Confirmed** no. *"What's MTA's real budget?"* → **conflict** surfaced (40M vs 52M). Majlis flags: don't cite an MTA number in the vote until it's reconciled. The DG walks in knowing the one thing to drive and the one number not to trust.

**During (in the committee).** As the agenda reaches EDD, Majlis surfaces the SSO risk and its dependents. EDD's rep claims recovery by Q3 — Majlis logs it as a *new* commitment. EKD's rep says "we're confident on the portal"; Majlis quietly **flags the inconsistency** with the August slip on record. The DG asks discreetly, *"remind me what HSA committed."* The committee defers the reallocation pending MTA reconciliation — Majlis **captures the decision, owner, and due date.**

**After (minutes later).** Majlis drafts the minutes: decisions (reallocation deferred), **new commitments** (EDD: SSO by end-Q3; MTA: reconcile in 2 weeks), action items + owners — each with a confidence rating. The DG edits, approves, sends. Those new commitments are written to institutional memory — so **next quarter's brief opens by checking whether EDD shipped SSO and whether MTA reconciled.** The loop closes.

## Feature inventory (broad — to be prioritized)
Tags: ① briefing · ② grounded Q&A · ③ confidence/sourcing · ④ risk/sensitivity · 🔁 feeds the loop

### Before — prep & rehearse
- **Briefing assembly** ① — at-a-glance: 5 entities, the decision, the headline risk. 🔁
- **Grounded Q&A** ② — ask anything over the pack, every claim cited.
- **Confidence & sourcing** ③ — Confirmed / Likely / Unverified + source drawer.
- **Risk & sensitivity flags** ④ — the can't-miss item (EDD SSO), conflicts (MTA), restricted (PSD).
- **Prior-commitment / consistency check** ④🔁 — what each entity committed last cycle and whether they delivered.
- **Who's in the room** ① — participant dossiers: role, prior commitments, track record.
- **Decision pre-read** ① — the reallocation: options, implications, what to verify first.
- **Anticipated questions / rehearsal** ② — what others may raise; your weak spots.
- **Open threads** 🔁 — unresolved action items carried from last meeting.
- **Share brief to participants** — the secondary "every participant" persona.

### During — live support
- **Discreet live Q&A** ② — quick in-room reference.
- **Agenda tracker** — where we are, what's next.
- **Real-time fact/risk surfacing** ④ — relevant facts appear as a topic comes up.
- **Live inconsistency flag** ④ — someone states something at odds with the record. ⭐ signature
- **Decision & commitment capture** 🔁 — structured who/what/by-when, as it happens. ⭐ signature
- **Confidence guardrail** ③ — about to cite a number? check it's Confirmed first.
- **Park-it** — capture open questions to resolve afterward.

### After — follow-through
- **Auto-drafted minutes** ①③ — grounded summary; confidence on decided vs. discussed. ⭐ signature
- **New commitments → institutional memory** 🔁 — written back as next cycle's prior positions. ⭐ signature (the loop)
- **Action items & owners** — extracted with due dates.
- **Decision record** — what, why, any dissent.
- **Follow-up / accountability** 🔁 — did owners deliver (checked next cycle).
- **Distribution** — send minutes to participants.
- **Discrepancy log** — unresolved unverified/conflicting items.
- **Audit trail** ③ — the DG's queries + what they acted on.

### Cross-cutting
- Confidence/sourcing everywhere · audit-awareness (logged) · multi-participant access · the institutional-memory loop as the spine.

## Recommended prioritization (a starting point — to confirm together)
- **Build deep (hero):** all of **Before** ①②③④ incl. the prior-commitment/consistency check.
- **Signature (interactive, lighter):** During → *live inconsistency flag* + *commitment capture*; After → *auto-minutes that write commitments back* (the loop).
- **Sketch / context:** agenda tracker, who's-in-room dossiers, decision pre-read, follow-up tracking, distribution.
- **Cut:** reminders, full note-taking, accounts, real ingestion.

**Why this split:** deep *Before* exercises all four brief capabilities and is the most demoable; the two signature moments tell the multi-dimensional story and land the institutional-memory loop *without* over-building; everything else stays context. It honors the brief's "scope down — one or two interactions with real intention" while still flowing across the lifecycle.
