# 02 · Scenario Exploration

Seven candidate scenarios, grouped by the brief's three modes. Each was checked against four tests:
costly failure mode · sourcing is load-bearing · risk/consistency flag has teeth · scopeable in 5 days.

Template — **Who · Moment · Why wrong is costly · Sources · Capabilities (1–4) · DGE product line.**

## Mode 1 — The Meeting
**1. The bilateral you can't afford to misread** · *internal tooling / reporting*
Under-Secretary prepping the Minister 30 min before a bilateral with a visiting delegation. Reaffirming a commitment that has quietly shifted — or getting a name/title/MOU wrong — is a protocol failure in front of the counterpart. Sources: prior minutes, signed MOUs, the counterpart's public statements, trade data. Showcases ① briefing + ④ risk flags.

**2. The submission before the room** · *reporting / internal tooling*
Abu Dhabi Executive Council member with one hour to form a view on a policy/capital submission. Approving something that contradicts a prior council decision is on the record. Showcases ① briefing + ② grounded Q&A + ③ confidence.

## Mode 2 — The Decision
**3. The signature you'll have to defend** (procurement award sign-off) · *procurement* ⭐ strongest DGE-product fit
Procurement committee chair must sign off a major tender award. A wrong award is auditable and challengeable — conflicts of interest, price anomalies, a vendor that underdelivered. Sources: evaluation scores, bid docs, vendor history, prior awards, conflict register, budget. Showcases ④ risk flags + ② grounded Q&A + ③ confidence. Cleanest "cannot act on unverified" framing and the most believable dataset to simulate.

**4. The name you put forward** · *recruitment / HR*
Official approving a senior appointment. Gaps/inconsistencies surfacing post-appointment. On-mandate but more sensitive; fairness concerns dominate.

## Mode 3 — The Public Appearance
**5. The question you didn't see coming** (scrutiny prep) · *reporting* ⭐ highest craft ceiling
Minister prepping the night before a scrutiny session. A contradiction with a prior *public* statement becomes the headline; citing an unverified number publicly is damaging. Showcases ④ **consistency-with-prior-positions** + ③ confidence (public/confirmed vs. internal/unverified) + ② likely-question Q&A.

**6. On the record in 20 minutes** (press conference) · *reporting*
Minister walking into a press conference after an announcement. Showcases ③ **"confidence-to-speak"** (say / caveat / don't) + ④ landmine flags + ② rehearse likely questions. "Confidence-to-speak" is a crisp, novel AI-state to design.

**7. The stage and the narrative** (summit panel) · *reporting*
Leader on a panel at a global summit. Showcases the UAE's posture but is broad and hardest to bound in 5 days.

---

### First-pass recommendation (later revised)
Initially recommended **#5/#6 (public-appearance)** for the highest craft ceiling, or **#3 (procurement)** for the tightest DGE fit. See `03` for how adding **likelihood** changed the ranking, and `05` (D1) for the final call.
