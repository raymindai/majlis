# 03 · Selection Lenses and Re-score

The scenarios were judged through four lenses, added over two passes.

## The lenses
- **Impact** — how costly is "wrong," how visible is the AI's value, how often does the moment recur.
- **Product platform** — the *moment of use* dictates form factor. Prep-on-the-move → tablet/phone, glanceable. Deep desk review → desktop, dense, document-anchored. All of it stays a **web app** (fastest with Claude Code, instantly demoable, matches DGE's stack).
- **Presentation fit** — does the scenario have a clean narrative arc, and does the *medium* itself prove an AI-first way of working.
- **Likelihood** *(added second pass — user's steer)* — how *real and recurring* is the moment, and how well does it fit an **Abu Dhabi executive** org specifically.

## The re-score (with likelihood)
| Scenario | Impact | Likelihood | On-mandate fit | Craft ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B. Procurement award sign-off | High | **Very high** | **Very high** (named in JD) | High |
| A. Public-appearance prep | Very high | **Medium** | Medium (press/scrutiny is federal) | Very high |
| E. Council submission review | High | **High** | High | Medium (fuzzy data) |
| C. Bilateral / delegation prep | High | Medium | Medium | High |

## The trade-off this exposed
- **Optimize for defensible realism** → procurement (B): most likely, most on-mandate, cleanest dataset, airtight "cannot act on unverified."
- **Optimize for craft showcase** → public-appearance (A): highest demo drama and the most novel AI-states, but lower likelihood / more federal framing.

Claude's revised recommendation was **B**. **The user chose A** and reframed its likelihood: the briefing pattern is **not limited to senior officials — every meeting participant can use it**, which (in the user's view) makes it more broadly real than a single-principal moment. → see `05` (D1).

## Platform & presentation conclusions (carried forward)
- **Platform:** build a **web app**, designed to a deliberate form factor that fits the chosen moment (tablet for on-the-move briefing; desktop for deep review). Exact call pending the persona decision.
- **Presentation:** **AI-built microsite + embedded short demo**, because the medium proves the AI-first skill. Guardrail: don't let it eat the prototype's time.
