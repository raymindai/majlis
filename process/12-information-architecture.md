# 12 · Information Architecture — hierarchy & data flow

The three style variants shared a weak skeleton: a flat two-column layout, brief and Q&A as disconnected boxes, five entities treated as equals, no dominant element. **Style isn't hierarchy.** Fix the structure first; visual skin comes after.

## Start from the primary audience
**The Programme Director-General.** Senior, accountable, ~15–20 minutes before a high-stakes committee, about to make and defend a decision. Time-poor. Their cognitive task isn't "browse five entities" — it's:

> "What am I walking into, what must I decide, what needs my attention, and what can I trust vs. check?"

So the design must **triage for them** — lead with judgment, rank by exception, let them drill — not hand them a flat data dump.

## Reading order = the data flow
① **Bottom line** → ② **Your decision** → ③ **Needs attention (ranked)** → ④ **Interrogate (on demand)** — a single narrative column, top-down. Plus a provenance micro-flow that repeats everywhere: **claim → confidence → source → exact passage.** Plus the lifecycle spine: **Before ▸ During ▸ After**, with open items carrying forward and commitments looping back.

## Hierarchy (what dominates)
- **PRIMARY (hits first):** the bottom-line judgment (the "so what") + the decision at stake. Largest type, top, unmissable.
- **SECONDARY:** what needs attention — the exceptions, ranked (e.g. 3 of 5 entities), each with its trust signal.
- **TERTIARY / on demand:** on-track + restricted entities collapsed; the Q&A input (the door to depth); full source text in a drawer.

## What this fixes
- **Clear hierarchy:** one dominant element (the bottom line), then ranked layers — not five equal cards.
- **Clear data flow:** a single reading path ①→④; brief and Q&A become one flow (asking = drilling into the brief), not two boxes; provenance is a consistent micro-flow.
- **Audience-true:** decision/risk-led triage for a time-poor DG, not a flat dashboard.

## Extends to the other stages
- **During:** same spine; "needs attention" becomes the live watch-list; inconsistencies surface inline; commitments captured.
- **After:** the flow inverts — captured decisions/commitments compose the minutes, which write back to become the next Before's "prior commitments."

## Then — and only then — the visual skin
Institutional / Situation / Dossier become *skins* on this skeleton. The structure is the design; the skin is taste.
